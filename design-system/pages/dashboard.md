# Dashboard page override

The dashboard is an operational scan surface. It should prioritise the administrator's reading flow over forcing every detail above the fold.

- Keep the existing three-tier hierarchy: KPI cards, revenue trend with the work queue, and capacity with decision support.
- Use white surfaces, quiet mint ambient detail, teal as the main operational colour, and low-saturation blue only for a genuinely meaningful financial comparison.
- The finance chart uses teal for net income. Show the thin dashed blue gross-revenue reference only when it differs from net income; refunds remain small red exception markers.
- The work queue stays a fixed, readable card: show six items per page and use small pagination controls rather than increasing its height or adding a nested scroll area.
- Decorative halos and dot fields sit behind content only. They are intentionally low-contrast and never change layout on hover.
